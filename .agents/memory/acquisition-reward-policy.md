---
name: Acquisition reward policy
description: Business reward timing and stable public business links.
---
Referral credits and door-hanger employee bonuses default to the first paid invoice, not customer signup or estimate acceptance.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose first paid invoice as the default reward trigger.

**How to apply:** Keep rewards separate from deposits and normal wages; preserve earned reward history when company reward settings change.

Business portal slugs remain stable when the business display name changes.

**Why:** Renaming a company should not break customer bookmarks or previously shared marketing links.

**How to apply:** Allocate a business-name slug once; any future slug-change feature needs redirects rather than silently replacing existing links.
