---
name: Billing coverage and prepaid credits
description: All completed work must be accounted for without double billing prepaid or plan-covered work.
---
Completed jobs must have invoice coverage or visible admin attention. Prepaid customers still receive invoices; available prepaid funds are applied separately from gross invoice revenue.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked that no completed jobs disappear from invoicing and that admins be alerted when billing is missed.

**How to apply:** Keep recurring work on its billing cycle. Missing pricing, holds, and uncertain plan coverage need explicit review, not silent omission or duplicate per-visit charges. Recovery invoices are drafts. General account prepayments and estimate deposits are separate: never count the same deposit twice. Reserve credits transactionally on draft creation so concurrent invoices cannot spend the same balance, and reverse reserved credits when cancelling.
