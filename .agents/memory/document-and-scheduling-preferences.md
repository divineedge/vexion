---
name: Document organization and rescheduling choices
description: User requirements for invoice and estimate access, reusable recurring billing, previews, defaults, and explicit fees.
---

Invoices and Estimates are adjacent one-click navigation destinations. Schedule, Visits, and Daily Tasks do not belong under Customers.

**Why:** The user repeatedly found billing controls and document previews hard to locate.

**How to apply:** Keep basic defaults in separate Invoicing and Estimates settings; place document editing, templates, customer previews, and printed previews alongside the documents.

Recurring billing rules should be reusable across customers rather than requiring a template for each customer. Notes and terms inherit company defaults, can be overridden or explicitly suppressed, and are snapshotted onto documents.

**Why:** The user wants recurring invoices to work like regular invoices without duplicated customer-specific setup or changes to previously issued wording.

**How to apply:** Shared completed-work rules generate separate customer invoices. Null inherits wording; blank suppresses it. Defaults and template changes must not rewrite existing documents.

Every drag-and-drop or manual reschedule asks whether to charge a rescheduling fee before saving; cancel leaves the appointment and price unchanged.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested the choice, not an automatic fee.

**How to apply:** Confirm the amount, save the date and fee together, and do not interpret the choice as permission to charge a card.
