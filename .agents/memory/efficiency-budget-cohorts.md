---
name: Comparable budget efficiency
description: Keep employee and crew budget efficiency based on the same completed jobs and contributing labor.
---
Budget efficiency compares the full actual labor on jobs completed in the selected period against those jobs' budgets. Allocate a job's budget among actual workers proportionally to their recorded labor, rather than assigning the entire budget only to the named assignee.

**Why:** The user found that a one-person crew showed higher efficiency than its only worker. Assignee-only budgets and timer-start-date filtering were comparing different work.

**How to apply:** Include timers from before the report period when their jobs finished in it. Unbudgeted jobs keep their actual labor visible but do not contribute to the budget-efficiency denominator. Crew totals must use budgeted-job labor too. Clock utilization is a separate date-window measure.
