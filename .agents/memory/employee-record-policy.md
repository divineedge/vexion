---
name: Employee records and hiring policy
description: User-selected private HR access, hiring channels, and employee evidence boundaries.
---
Private HR and hiring records belong to the business owner and explicitly designated HR staff, not every office manager. Applications must support both a website application link and staff entry.

**Why:** The user chose owner/designated-HR access and both hiring channels when requesting employee documentation, yellow cards, performance records, and hiring.

**How to apply:** Preserve these choices when adding employee or applicant features. Sharing an employee record must be deliberate; assigning a staff role alone does not grant HR access.

Hiring and corrective actions remain human decisions. Clock-event GPS is recorded evidence, not continuous tracking or proof of presence. An acknowledgment means seen, not agreement. A signature or acknowledgment must not automatically be presented as agreement or legal certification.

**Why:** The requested records may support employee corrections or termination review; do not turn incomplete operational data into automatic employment decisions or claims of legal sufficiency.

**How to apply:** Preserve originals, form wording, and evidence snapshots; corrections and sharing changes must be auditable. A new hire should not acquire an active login or HR privileges automatically.
