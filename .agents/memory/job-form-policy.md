---
name: Job form policy
description: Required custom job forms and historical template safety.
---
Custom job forms must be visible to staff, reusable as templates, and optionally required by the company.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested all three capabilities.

**How to apply:** The initial enforcement point is job completion, not starting the clock: work-related answers may only be known after the work. Keep existing job questions and submitted answers when templates are edited or archived.
