---
name: P4P damage responsibility
description: Employee-specific damage adjustments, approval, hourly protection and period boundaries.
---

Damage reports belong in P4P and must be attached to the responsible employee or employees. The user chose deductions directly from P4P earnings, not from job revenue, and manually assigned dollar amounts for each employee rather than an automatic equal split.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected “From P4P earnings” and “Set each employee’s amount.”

**How to apply:** Require an authorized payroll reviewer’s reason and approval before a report affects earnings. Preserve guaranteed hourly pay and unrelated acquisition bonuses; never infer responsibility simply from a job assignment or maintenance concern. Employee-file visibility follows private HR access, while employees may see only their own allocation.

Apply approved amounts to the selected payroll period, cap net P4P at zero, and do not automatically carry unused damage amounts into later periods. Finalization must resolve pending reports and must not bypass an unknown hourly guarantee through P4P-only authorization. Finalized reports stay immutable; later corrections are separate audited payroll adjustments.

**Why:** An approved report is not authorization to reduce guaranteed wages or deduct from future payroll indefinitely. Existing payroll history must survive later changes.

**How to apply:** Keep the approved allocation, actual gross-pay reduction and hourly-floor protection distinguishable. Payroll preview, finalization and employee views must use the same calculation.
