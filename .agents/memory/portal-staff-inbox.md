---
name: Portal staff inbox
description: Admin access and independent unread tracking for customer portal communications.
---
Owners and staff with Admin/Office Manager access must be able to see customer portal messages and service requests.

**Why:** The user reported messages appearing only for the owner and explicitly requires admins to handle this work too.

**How to apply:** Keep access tenant-scoped and role-checked on the server, not just in navigation. A conversation opened by one admin must not clear another admin's unread queue. Distinguish each staff member's personal unread state from the customer-facing receipt that someone at the business has seen the message.
