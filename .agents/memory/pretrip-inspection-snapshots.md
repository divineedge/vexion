---
name: Pre-trip inspection snapshots
description: Editable pre-trip questions and media evidence must preserve historical inspection context.
---

**The rule:** Pre-trip checklist wording and answers are immutable snapshots on each saved equipment inspection; photos and videos attach to the inspection record, not directly to equipment.

**Why:** Checklist templates can change over time, and evidence must remain attributable to the exact equipment inspection performed that day.

**How to apply:** Resolve the current tenant checklist in the Field App, save labels and answers with the selected equipment, then register uploaded evidence against the resulting inspection identifier.
