---
name: Pre-trip inspection snapshots
description: Editable pre-trip questions and media evidence must preserve historical inspection context.
---

**The rule:** Pre-trip and optional end-of-day checklist wording and answers are immutable snapshots on each saved equipment inspection; photos and videos attach to the inspection record, not directly to equipment. End-of-day is enabled only when its separate tenant checklist has questions.

**Why:** Checklist templates can change over time, and evidence must remain attributable to the exact equipment inspection performed that day.

**How to apply:** Resolve the current checklist by inspection type in the Field App, save server-authoritative labels and answers with the selected equipment, then register uploaded evidence against the resulting inspection identifier.
