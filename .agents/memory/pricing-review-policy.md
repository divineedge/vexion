---
name: Pricing review policy
description: Historical job pricing advice belongs outside Budget and must not rewrite past prices.
---

The user wants a separate report of underpriced jobs using recorded person-hours and saved Budget targets, with a configurable percentage threshold for reviewing a whole service matrix.

**Why:** They want Budget to guide pricing decisions without putting this report on the Budget tab.

**How to apply:** Recommendations are advisory, never automatic price changes. Exclude missing prices, incomplete time, and unreliable material snapshots from comparisons. Apply the division's minimum labor visit charge before adding material selling prices.

Budget divisions should be tied to services so Pricing Review automatically pulls the applicable budget targets, including the minimum show-up charge.

**Why:** The user requested service-linked budget guidance and reported that the minimum was missing. Company-level comparisons must not silently waive the minimum shown in Budget.

**How to apply:** Keep assignments within each budget year so reorganizing a later year's divisions does not alter earlier years' links. Use the service's division by default; label company fallback for unassigned services and allow explicit review overrides. Apply the minimum to labor before adding materials, and leave historical prices unchanged.
