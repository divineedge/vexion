---
name: Pricing review policy
description: Historical job pricing advice belongs outside Budget and must not rewrite past prices.
---

The user wants a separate report of underpriced jobs using recorded person-hours and saved Budget targets, with a configurable percentage threshold for reviewing a whole service matrix.

**Why:** They want Budget to guide pricing decisions without putting this report on the Budget tab.

**How to apply:** Recommendations are advisory, never automatic price changes. Exclude missing prices, incomplete time, and unreliable material snapshots from comparisons. Apply the division's minimum labor visit charge before adding material selling prices.
