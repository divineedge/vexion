---
name: Property invoice billing
description: Customer choice for combined versus separate property billing.
---
Jobs and invoices must support property selection. Work from multiple properties belonging to the same customer should be combined on one invoice by default.

**Why:** The user wants one invoice even when the customer has two or more properties, unless the customer requests separate billing.

**How to apply:** Offer a saved customer choice for separate invoices per property and an explicit choice when creating an invoice. Apply the saved choice to future recurring billing without changing its frequency. Identify each property's work on combined documents, and preserve the customer's billing choice when other payment settings are edited.
