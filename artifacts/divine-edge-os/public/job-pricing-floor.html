How it works
Labor = wage × (1 + burden %) × crew × all clock hours.
Overhead share = monthly overhead ÷ monthly billable crew hours × on-site hours.
Price = total stop cost ÷ (1 − margin %), rounded up to a cent.

Driving is paid, but overhead is recovered only through on-site hours so it is not allocated twice. Use realistic seasonal billable hours and include all costs. Sales tax is not profit and is not included. This is a planning estimate, not a guarantee of profitability.
